What is Vertical Farming?

Vertical farming grows plants in stacked layers, often indoors. Instead of depending entirely on traditional fields, farms can control light, water, nutrients and temperature.

💧

Water Efficiency

Hydroponic systems can recirculate water, helping reduce water use compared with many conventional growing methods.

🏢

Grow Vertically

Stack growing trays to make better use of available floor space in urban and indoor environments.

🌞

Smart Climate

Monitor light, humidity and temperature to create suitable growing conditions for different crops.

Technology That Helps Plants Thrive

Different growing methods make vertical farming adaptable to different spaces, crops and budgets.

🌊

Hydroponics

Plants grow without soil, using a nutrient-rich water solution and a suitable support system.

🌫️

Aeroponics

Plant roots are suspended and supplied with a fine mist of water and nutrients.

💡

LED Grow Lights

Adjustable lighting can provide plants with the light spectrum and duration needed for growth.

What Can You Grow?

Leafy greens and herbs are common starting points for indoor farms. Crop choice depends on lighting, operating costs and market demand.

🥬 Lettuce

A popular crop for hydroponic systems because of its compact growth and relatively short growing cycle.

Beginner friendly

🌿 Basil & Herbs

Herbs can work well in controlled environments when light, airflow and nutrients are managed properly.

High-value potential

🥗 Microgreens

Young edible greens harvested early. They can be suitable for small growing spaces and local markets.

Short growing cycle
